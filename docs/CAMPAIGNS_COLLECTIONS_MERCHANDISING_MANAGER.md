# Campaigns, Collections & Merchandising Manager

Aloyri Ecommerce Admin now owns storefront merchandising while Aloyri CRM remains the source of truth for selling price, promotion eligibility, stock and orders.

## Collections
- Create active/inactive curated collections
- Manual product selection and ordering
- Per-collection out-of-stock behavior
- Collection media
- Collection landing pages under /collections/<slug>
- Collection SEO metadata and social image
- Collection sitemap inclusion only when active/indexable

## Campaigns
- Draft-controlled campaign creative
- Start/end scheduling in Bangladesh time
- CTA label and internal destination
- Collection and direct-product targeting
- Campaign media and saved-draft preview
- Campaign-specific out-of-stock behavior
- Promotion-only mode that filters to products with a CRM-authoritative sale price
- Automatic runtime activation/deactivation after the campaign has been published

## Product merchandising
- New, Bestseller, Limited, Staff Pick and Trending website badges
- Manual merchandising priority
- Per-product out-of-stock override
- CRM promotion badges always take precedence over website badges

## Homepage & shop
- Hero product selection
- Ordered homepage sections for Bestsellers, Featured, New Arrivals, Collections and Campaigns
- Global out-of-stock behavior
- Shop priority-vs-featured default ordering
- Live CRM catalog continues to refresh price and stock

## Safety
- All changes are Draft-only until Publish
- Version History and restore automatically include merchandising state
- Health checks detect missing/inactive/out-of-stock CRM targets, bad schedules, duplicate collection slugs and broken homepage references
- Price, sale price, promotion eligibility, stock and order workflow cannot be edited by this manager
